Privacy Policy

Last Revised: August 2026

Back to Homepage
Commitment to User Transparency: KavyaHub operates as a non-commercial educational discovery index. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal identity to third parties.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the web platform KavyaHub (accessible via kavyahub.vercel.app and associated domains). It describes how visitor information is handled, our usage of web technologies, and the measures taken to safeguard your browsing experience.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint. We categorize information into two types:

  • Voluntary Information: If you reach out to us via email or the support contact form, we collect your name, email address, and inquiry details solely to respond to your request.
  • Automated Log Data: Standard server logs record anonymous technical metadata (browser user-agent, operating system, timestamp, and referring URL) to diagnose routing bugs and optimize platform latency.

3. Cookies, Web Beacons & Ad Networks

KavyaHub may integrate third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to sustain platform hosting:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads based on browsing habits across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out Page or through Google Ads Settings.

4. External Outbound Links & Third-Party Platforms

KavyaHub provides curated external links to third-party web portals, including YouTube tutorials, official university scholarship registries, and government employment boards. Once you click an outbound link and leave our domain, you are subject to the independent privacy terms and cookie practices of that target provider.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (User Rights)

In accordance with global data protection frameworks (including EU GDPR and California CCPA), you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Erasure / Access: You can request a summary or complete deletion of any voluntary correspondence by emailing us.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not restrict access to educational content based on privacy preferences.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

Our educational resources are designed for students, high-schoolers, and young professionals. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe such data has been transmitted, please notify us immediately for swift deletion.
