Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about KavyaHub.

Are all resources and certificates on KavyaHub free?

Yes. We prioritize 100% free courses, government-supported certifications, and open-source learning paths. Whenever an opportunity has optional paid tracks, it is explicitly labeled.

How often is the platform updated with new internships and jobs?

Our team updates the portal continuously. Check the Timeline Calendar to review opportunities published on any given date.

What should I do if a link is broken or expired?

Use the inquiry form above with the subject "Report Broken Link" and provide the title or link. Our moderation team will update or archive the record promptly.
